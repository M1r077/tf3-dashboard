Music for the camera travelling (Map tab > Camera views > Travelling).

Drop your own .mp3 / .ogg / .m4a / .wav / .flac files in this folder: a random one plays with every travelling
(default "Any"); the "Music" row of the travelling panel lets you pick one track or switch music off. The track is played by the browser on the dashboard screen (the game has no "play this file"
API), fades in over 2 s and out over the last 3 s of the travelling, and stops with it. Nothing is uploaded or
copied anywhere; the files stay on your PC and are not part of the release zip.
