## Companion 0.10.0 - 13 October 2026 (mod revision 18)

Mod revision 18 brings the new camera motion and the heading fix; the companion keeps working with revision 17 (the pan cap and the terrain fix are companion-side).

- Mod: camera travellings follow textbook motion rules instead of per-leg tweaks. The waypoint times are the knots of the Catmull-Rom spline (Barry-Goldman form, Yuksel-Schaefer-Keyser 2011) on the ground point, the distance, the heading and the pitch: the speed is continuous through every waypoint (it jumped by a factor of three wherever a short approach leg met a long stretch). One speed profile for the whole path: minimum-jerk quintic ramps (Flash & Hogan 1985) of at most 3 s at both ends, constant speed between, none on a loop. Heading and pitch go through a 0.6 s first-order filter fed with the spline 0.6 s ahead, so a change of turning rate is rounded off and the final frame lands exactly on the last point. Bug fixed on the way: the line tour and the terrain clearance used the heading convention the wrong way round (the eye at `(sin a, -cos a)` instead of looking along `(cos a, -sin a)`, the convention checked against the compass in 0.8.1 for the map cone): the tour looked across the track instead of along it in half the directions, and the clearance sampled the ground on the wrong side of the target, hence dives into hills despite the 60 m margin. Default tour speed alt/16 in the mod too (the dashboard already sent it). When a travelling or a vehicle view starts, the game's data-layer ridge (the eleven toggles: passenger flow, satisfaction, noise...) unfolds with nothing selected, so the player can study a layer while the camera flies; it folds again when the travelling ends or is stopped from the dashboard, and stays as it is when the player takes the camera.
- Dashboard: the pan speed is capped at 0.1 rad/s, the cinematographers' rule of one frame width per 7 s (RED, "Panning speed best practices") with the game's ~40 degree field of view. The chosen duration is a minimum: a full orbit takes about 63 s whatever the button says, a sweep x1 (45 degrees out and back) at least 32 s, and the duration row shows "-> 63 s" when the cap applies. Line tours (3.5 degrees/s) are unaffected. One `camLook()` for the cone and the eye track on the map, mirrored in the mod.
- Collector: the bottom of the map kept the coarse relief after a game launch ("20/22 bands" in the legend, for good). The mod had written all 22 band files; the collector, started seconds before, was reading the whole folder in one slow pass (forty leftover band files of earlier maps, half a minute of decoding) while the mod rewrote band 0, 1, 2... A fresh band 0 was accepted, then slot 2 still held the previous map's content, its other `revs` reset the assembler, and band 0 was dropped with its file time recorded as read, so it never came back. Three changes: a band older than the running export by more than the export gap is ignored *before* the reset tests, whatever its revs; leftover files older than the newest band by that gap are not decoded at all on a first pass (marked seen, a rewrite brings them back); and when bands are still missing a minute after the last one arrived, every band file is re-read once for that export. Test in `migrate_check.py` (fails on 0.9.0).

## Companion 0.9.0 - 13 October 2026 (mod revision 17)

Mod revision 17 is required for the crash fix below; the companion keeps working with revision 16 (the broken-line marker simply never shows).

- Mod: removing a station, harbour or stop still served by a line crashed the game a few seconds later (reported on mod.io, reproduced: `Engine.h GetComponentDataIndex: Assertion 'it != components.end()' failed`). The culprit is `checkLineStopForVehicleOverlength` on the orphaned stop: the station group entity survives the bulldozer (the line still references it, the game shows the line in red) while the station inside it is gone, and the engine asserts when it walks the terminals, a fatal error `pcall` cannot catch. A stop is now "whole" only when its group and every station of the group exist; an orphaned stop gets no engine call at all, and its line is exported bare (id, name, colour, vehicles, stop list; no capacities, headway, quality or terminals) until the player deletes or replaces the stop. Also: every `getComponent` goes through an `entityExists` check, a slow-section item whose entity vanished since the section began is skipped.
- Dashboard: such a line shows a red warning after its name (table and detail), a one-line explanation in the detail, the orphaned stop struck out in the stop table (no station link or camera for it) and in the stop sequence; it counts as a problem for the "problems only" filter. `line_stop.gone` column.
- Stations bulldozed, industries closed and removed, depots demolished stayed in the Stations, Industries and Depots tabs, on the map and in a town's "served" count forever, with their last state. Same treatment as deleted lines (0.8.3): the collector marks an entity missing from a complete export as gone (`station.gone`, `industry.gone`, `depot.gone`; the row stays so the history keeps the name and position), the dashboard shows only the living ones. A cycle whose section failed in the mod marks nothing; an entity exported again (older save reloaded) comes back.
