# Changelog

Mod revision and companion version are released together (see README, Install). Public notes on mod.io carry the latest entry only.

## Unreleased

Served / not served filter on the towns, industries and stations tables (a three-way switch, remembered per browser), with a name filter on towns and stations. A town counts as served when a line calls at one of its stations - the game's "stations" figure includes stations nothing stops at, so the column now reads calling/built. The industries checkbox becomes "closing only". One look for every toolbar switch: the map layer toggles (three groups: terrain, objects, data layers) and the table checkboxes (worn, problems, closing) are drawn like the segmented controls, a lit segment meaning "shown".

## Revision 16 + companion 0.7.1 - 12 October 2026

Revision 16 + companion program 0.7.1 (12 October 2026): what a freight station can load, a ruler that finds the road, tables that keep their headers. In TF3 only passengers wait at a station - freight sits in the piles of the industries in reach until a vehicle calls, which is why freight stations always showed 0: the mod now exports the industries in each station's catchment area and the dashboard shows what can be loaded there (cargo icons with the pile amounts, in the table and per industry in the detail with a link). The ruler no longer answers "not linked" for towns the road does join: it routes over one connected piece of network, the one reached with the least building. Tables keep their title and column headers while the rows scroll. Also: vehicle icons in dark line colours made readable on the map, a "views" toggle for the camera pins, a steeper dimming of the cargo layers, camera panels that keep their scroll across refreshes, per-cargo waiting chips in the stations table. Revision 16 needs companion 0.7.1 for the "can load" chips; older companions keep working. https://github.com/M1r077/tf3-dashboard/releases/latest

Stations table: the waiting column shows the per-cargo chips of the detail instead of the bare total.

What a freight station can load (mod rev 16 + companion): in TF3 only passengers wait at a station - freight sits in the piles of the industries in reach until a vehicle calls, which is why freight stations always showed 0. The mod now exports the stock lists in each station's catchment area; the table shows "can load" chips (cargo icon + amount in the piles) and the detail lists each industry in reach with its piles and a link. Passenger stations get a grey "in transit" chip when the station counts more people than wait (boarding, moving between platforms). Freight stations no longer show a 0 waiting, a dash occupancy or a 0 overflow: the game has no such notions for them.

Map: a "views" toggle hides the saved camera pins and the travelling track. Cargo layers: steeper dimming ramp (grey = nothing happens yet, full = need met / production at its maximum; a need served at a third stays close to grey). Camera views, travellings and workshop keep their scroll across refreshes when the card is sized smaller than its content. Vehicle icons on the map: a dark line colour (navy, forest green) is lifted to a readable brightness, the hue kept; swatches and tables keep the exact colour.

Tables: the card title and the column headers stay put while the rows scroll (a later style rule had cancelled the sticky headers). The stations page no longer fails on a database the collector has not migrated yet (offline planning on an old copy).

Ruler: the road and rail proposals no longer disappear into "not linked: build straight" when both points are in towns the network does join. A network is many islands (an industry's access road, a depot siding, a town only reached by rail); each end used to be projected on whatever segment was nearest, often such an island. Both ends are now projected on the same connected piece of network, the one they reach with the least building, and the route is drawn over it.

## Revision 15 + companion 0.7.0 - 11 October 2026

Revision 15 + companion program 0.7.0 (11 October 2026): what is waiting at a station, by cargo. The station detail now lists what waits there - passengers, coal, goods... - with the count and, in red, how many have waited too long, the game's own quality figures (`getCargoQualityDataAtStation`) read for the cargos of the lines serving the station only, so the export costs the game a few milliseconds per cycle. Detail panels get icons: the capacity of a station splits into platforms and storage with the game's icons, overflow and workers rows too. Finances journal: the carrier rows (Road, Rail, Water...) no longer scroll away under the figures in Firefox (reported by violetV-). And three nets so a database problem can no longer go unnoticed (below). Revision 15 needs companion 0.7.0 for the waiting-by-cargo list; older companions keep working. https://github.com/M1r077/tf3-dashboard/releases/latest

After the 0.6.1-0.6.4 silent failure, three nets so a database problem can no longer go unnoticed: the collector adds at start-up any column `schema.sql` declares and the database lacks (and says so), instead of failing on every insert; `test\migrate_check.py` opens old databases with the current code, ingests a recorded export and fails the release build when a table stays empty or a migration is missing; and while `db\collector_errors.log` has been written in the last 24 h, the collector says so at start-up and the dashboard shows a red bar with the count, the time of the last failure and the file path.

## Companion 0.6.4 - 11 October 2026 (mod 14 unchanged)

Collector fix, important for anyone whose database was created before 0.6.1: the `boosters` column added then was missing from the migrations, so on such a database every slow cycle failed (lines, towns, stations, industries, depots were not recorded since 0.6.1 - the live part kept working, which hid it; `db/collector_errors.log` shows "table industry_state has 10 columns but 11 values were supplied"). The column is now added on start-up and every insert names its columns. Forget a save: deleted everything referencing the save (geo, finance journal, line paths were missed and the deletion failed on a foreign key; errors are now reported in the collector console).

Start-up with the game closed: the collector says at once which save the dashboard shows and how old the last export is; the server pane stamps the icon extraction (20-40 s on first start); the page and the Savegames panel say "server starting" and retry instead of a red error. Charts: cargo pictures in the legend and tooltip of the industry and finance charts. Ruler: carrier icons on the road / rail rows, the box no longer sits over the legend.

Game closed: the dashboard shows the last recorded situation of the save and says so in an amber bar under the header (save, game date, how long ago); the game-control buttons are off and commands are refused instead of piling up in a file nobody reads. With several saves in the database, Settings > Savegames gets a "View" button per save to look at another one offline; the live game takes over again as soon as it writes. Every "latest snapshot" lookup on the server is now scoped to the shown save.

Settings > Savegames: a "Forget" button per save deletes everything recorded for it (history, camera views, travellings; the savegame itself is untouched) - the collector does the deletion and compacts the file; the panel shows the database size on disk. The offline bar also says when the save was recorded and how many snapshots it holds. Collector: migrations and --forget-game no longer run their own blocking VACUUM, the hourly compaction reclaims the space.

Line detail: the link from a stop now opens its station (it carried the station group id and landed on the Stations tab without a selection).

The database stays small and the history stays for good: beyond the 2-hour detail window the slow history (lines, towns, stations, industries, depots, ~20 KB per snapshot) is thinned to one point per game day and then kept for as long as the save exists (it used to be kept in full for 14 days and then purged). Per-vehicle minutes are kept for 7 days (the fleet and finance minutes stay), and the collector compacts the file once an hour (WAL truncated, VACUUM when a quarter of the file is free). A 0.5 GB database was reported; the same data takes a few megabytes. Operations keeps its charts when no detail is left (after a long pause) by reading the minute aggregates. Travellings: a saved chain keeps its own list of views (it was dropped on save and fell back to every view).

## Mod 14 / companion 0.6.1 - 10 October 2026

Revision 14 needs companion 0.6.1 for the terrain, the piles and the boosters; older companions keep working.

Full-resolution terrain on the map: the mod exports the game's 4 m heightmap tiles once per load (22 s in the background, ~0.2 ms per frame), the collector assembles a 16-bit PNG, the map shades it with a new satellite style (rock by slope, meadows by altitude, water). Charts run on the game's simulation clock (x axis in game time, ticks by game month, or -6 months ... now while the calendar is paused). One timeline per save, like the game: reloading an older savegame deletes what was recorded beyond it (snapshots and minute aggregates); a one-shot cleanup applies the rule to existing databases and compacts them. Game-time ranges are one indexed query. The range bar is five buttons, one per question: since play (after the last pause or reload), 1 month, 1 year, 5 years, all; the real-minute ranges are gone (old links map to the nearest game range). Database audit: 46 columns and 3 views that no page ever showed are no longer stored and are dropped from existing databases at start-up. Shores at 4 m, the ruler estimates travel times from the fastest vehicle you own by carrier, the cargo rows on the map put the biggest owners first. Industry piles: the mod exports what lies in each stock (the figure of the industry window); the map's stock layer and the industry card show them. Detail cards (vehicle, line, town, industry, station) show every figure the database holds: top speed, running cost, value, days in depot / at terminal, nearest town; headway, transported per year, on line, ratings; district occupancy, growth, traffic, noise, pollution; piles, thrown away, closure; station group. Stops table: items waiting at each stop for the line, per cargo, red when some are unhappy. Fix: stations were labelled "cargo" as soon as they could take cargo (most passenger stations can); the type now comes from what the terminals actually load, and a station handling both shows both. Fix: in layout edit mode the detail cards (vehicle, line, town, industry, station) lost their drag bar and resize handles at the next refresh. Depots on the map (position from the depot construction), with parked and incoming counts on hover. Fix: in a detail card with a user-set height, the line list shrank into a narrow scroller; lists keep their height and the card scrolls as a whole. Travellings reworked around the flow: three cards beside the map (camera views = subjects, workshop = the draft, its settings and the only Play / Stop / progress, travellings = the saved list); the pencil loads a saved travelling into the workshop (apply / cancel) instead of editing in place; the line sheet keeps one button that hands the line over. Icons: the camera manager's own play / stop / save / pencil / bin, and six drawn icons for the camera movements the game has no icon for; no game icon is used for another meaning any more. Music: "Game" plays a random game track instead of listing all 24. Finances: fleet by type. Industry boosters, as the game's "Boosters" card: each cargo booster rule (what it needs vs what lies in the piles, its factor, active or not) and the workers booster (workers who came recently vs the half of the workplaces it takes, the productivity applied, the factor it can reach); the status chip shows the active total (+50 %) or 0/n; in the Industries table it only appears when a booster is active. "Unserved" now means nothing of yours moved there this year (an output shipped, or an input delivered for an industry without outputs); the filter checkbox says "only". Layout audit: charts fill user-sized cards exactly (legend and x axis measured), the camera cards scroll their list, no block overflows at any size. A shipped default layout for every tab (first start and Reset); your own arrangement is kept as before. Fix: the six drawn camera-movement icons were missing from the release zip.

## Mod 11 / companion 0.5.0 - 9 October 2026

Revision 11 + companion program 0.5.0 (9 October 2026): the map gets the real terrain, lines follow the tracks, a Travellings panel, the game's soundtrack. The map now draws the geography of your savegame: shaded relief with an altitude ramp, sea, lakes and rivers with refined shores, every road and railway (bridges and tunnels marked), with four map styles (dark, night, atlas, paper). Lines are drawn along the network: the real path of each vehicle as it drives, and a predicted route for lines not visited yet; the line tour now flies the rails and roads instead of straight stop-to-stop. Camera views can be attached to a vehicle (recalled = follow it again with the same framing), and the camera now keeps 60 m above the highest terrain between it and its target, so a travelling never dives into a hill. New Travellings card: compose a travelling (around a view, a chain of views you order by drag and drop, or a line tour), tune movement, duration, altitude, loop and music, play it, save up to 20 per savegame and edit them in place. Music: besides your own files, the game's 24 soundtrack pieces are offered, read straight from the game's install. Clicking a vehicle on the map follows it. The geography export costs the game about 2 ms per frame for a second or two after a load. Revision 11 needs companion 0.5.0 for the new map and the travellings; older companions keep working. https://github.com/M1r077/tf3-dashboard/releases/latest

## Mod 9 / companion 0.3.2 - 8 October 2026

Revision 9 + companion program 0.3.2 (8 October 2026): required after the game's stability update (build 40420). Since that update the game only lets mods write to a few of its userdata folders, and the export folder this mod used until revision 8 (dashboard_export) is no longer one of them: the mod ran but nothing was written, the dashboard stayed empty. Revision 9 writes to userdata/towns_industries/tf3dash_*.lua instead, a folder the game allows; the files carry a prefix so they never mix with your own town/industry exports. Companion 0.3.2 reads the new location (and still understands the old one). Both are needed: update the mod here, download the companion from GitHub, reload your savegame. Nothing else changes; your history is kept.
